## The Fool

{

### The Clown

Climbing the corporate ladder is cosplay.

In Lord of the Mysteries (LOTM), humans drink potions to obtain occult powers, embarking on a pathway where power levels progress in a sequence from 9 to 0.
One of the key findings that the protagonist arrives at is that one should not "digest" the potion, but rather to "act" it for maximal effect.
In the context of LOTM, each pathway embodies some characteristics of tarot cards.
So to "act it out" pushes you to become the personification of some major Arcana, which is how you can advance to the next level.

I find that this is almost completely analogous to working in big tech.
You posture as the ideal engineer in your manager's eyes, giving code review comments when none are needed, taking projects and pumping up various metrics to look good in the next performance review.
You maximize behaviors that serve your KPI, minimize behaviours that don't.
After all, time is limited.
I find it performative, and off-putting.

I am not against competition.
But I am currently competing in something I have absolutely no interest in.

Perhaps this is simply the price to pay for a high salary.
I don't think the protagonist in LOTM is progressing through the pathway for the love of the game.
He chose his specific pathway based on his fellow transmigrator's regrets.
If we analyze this from Dr K's framework, he may be pushing through this advancement because it is simply his duty.
That's what he has to do in order to find out how to return to his own world.
Unfortunately, I do not feel this.

I am not ready to embody the Fool.
